# Rain-Sound Channel — File 2: Monetization Risk, Keywords, Concepts, Global, Contrarian (Parts 1, 3, 4)

Prepared 21 Oct 2026. Data labelled **[VidIQ]** was pulled by me on 21 Oct 2026 through VidIQ (25 of 26 credits used; I cannot pull more). VidIQ figures are estimates; "<750" means below its reporting floor, not zero. **[my judgment]** marks opinion and rule-of-thumb thresholds that no source backs.

## 0. The risk that decides the design: "inauthentic content"

YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to **inauthentic content** on 15 Jul 2025 and says such content was always ineligible for monetization; the policy text covers templated, mass-produced material and videos with minimal variation from one to the next [YouTube monetization policies]. I found no YouTube text that exempts ambient loop channels and none that bans them. Ten near-identical loops made from stock clips and shared CC0 audio sit close to the described pattern, so **monetization review risk is real and unquantified**. Views and watch hours can still accrue; the question is whether the channel is accepted into or kept in the Partner Program.

What lowers the risk **[my judgment]**:
1. **Original audio.** Record rain yourself (phone voice recorder at a window or under a tin/corrugated roof, 44.1 kHz or higher, 20–40 minutes per take). It is the strongest answer to the policy, and it avoids Content ID claims on shared files (Pixabay warns its audio can be registered in Content ID).
2. **No recycled loops across videos.** Each upload gets its own audio and its own scene.
3. **Fewer, more distinct uploads.** Do not publish 3 h / 8 h / 10 h cuts of the same loop as separate videos in the first month.
4. Be honest in the description about how it was recorded or sourced.
5. Check the actual threshold and status in Studio → Earn; I did not verify current requirements.

The competitors that show huge numbers mostly use their own recordings or original 3D (Zenspannung describes its microphones; The Guild of Ambience says "no AI"; New Bliss builds 3D scenes). Matching their method is the safer path than matching their output.

## 2. Part 1 — Ten concepts

Scene evidence comes from the all-time top results for "Regengeräusche 10 Stunden" and related searches [VidIQ]. These are English-titled giants, so they prove the scene works, **not** that German demand exists for that exact scene. Those demand checks are in §3.2.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Regen am Fenster \| 10 Stunden Regengeräusche zum Einschlafen \| Ohne Musik** | Slow rain streaks on glass, dim lamp or blurred night street. Notes:
- Title length: the German part of #1 is about 72 characters. Add " | Rain Sounds" only if it fits under 100.
- Description opening (German, copy-ready): *"Reine Regengeräusche – 10 Stunden, ohne Musik, ohne Sprache. Gleichmäßige Lautstärke, kein plötzlicher Donner. Aufgenommen: [Ort/Quelle]. Lizenz: [CC0/eigene Aufnahme]."* Add "Einschlafhilfe" once later in the text. **Avoid cure or treatment claims** ("heilt Schlaflosigkeit"); several giants make them, but they invite medical-claims problems **[my judgment]**.

## 3. Part 3 — Germany-first strategy

### 3.1 What the German competition looks like [VidIQ]
- **Zenspannung** (71.5K subs): 6-hour forest rain, 14.4M views since 2019, own field recordings with named microphones, sells MP3s, has a 12-hour version and playlists. This is the benchmark to differentiate from.
- **Relax Time** (15.9K subs): 60-minute thunder/rain, black screen, 9.9M views since 2019, one-line description.
- Searching "Regengeräusche 10 Stunden" for Germany returned mostly English-titled giants (Danny Louis 70M and 50M; The Relaxed Guy 43.8M; Relaxing White Noise 34.6M). **German-titled 10-hour videos are a gap**, but it may be a gap because the same views go to English-titled content.
- Breakout search (German, under 10K subscribers, "Regengeräusche Einschlafen"): 19 hits. Small-channel breakouts were **narrated sleep stories** ("Geschichte zum Einschlafen": 1.6K subs, 39.9K views on a 3 h video; 4.8K subs, 54–61K views on 90-min videos, labelled AI), not pure rain. The only rain item was a meditation with rain sounds (1.7K subs, 2.2K views). **I did not find a recent small-channel breakout for pure German rain loops.** That does not prove none exist (VidIQ matches titles only), but it is thin evidence for the plan.

### 3.2 What to pull in VidIQ yourself (free account)
Candidate keywords I did **not** pull: `regen sounds`, `regen geräusche`, `regengeräusche 10 stunden`, `regengeräusche schwarzer bildschirm`, `regengeräusche fenster`, `regen auto geräusche`, `regen zelt`, `regen blechdach`, `regen kaminfeuer`, `regengeräusche wald`, `regengeräusche zum lernen`, `nieselregen geräusche`.

For each, record: estimated monthly searches, competition, DE-country volume, and 30-day change. Decision rule **[my judgment]**:

| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| ≥ 3,000/month and competition ≤ 30 | Build a video around it |
| ≥ 3,000 and competition 30–45 | Build only with a distinct scene or original audio |
| Competition > 45, or < 750 | Skip |
| 30-day change ≤ −25% | Check for seasonality before investing |

**Finding beatable competitors (Outliers tool):** language `de`, long-form, published within 6 months, max 10,000 subscribers, breakout ≥ 3×; run once per concept keyword. A competitor counts as beatable **[my judgment]** if it has under 10K subs, at least one video at ≥ 20K views and ≥ 3× its channel average, runtime ≥ 1 hour, and a thumbnail/title style you can match. **A keyword with 3 or more such channels is a proven niche; 0–1 is unproven.**

### 3.3 Metadata and auto-dubbing
1. **Turn auto-dubbing off for the channel:** Studio → Settings → Channel → Advanced settings → untick "Allow automatic dubbing" [YouTube dubbing help]. A 10-hour video is ineligible anyway (over 120 minutes), but the setting also covers shorter videos you may upload later.
2. **Video language:** German. If Studio offers a "no linguistic content" option for the language field, use it; I could not confirm it exists.
3. **Title:** primary keyword first (first about 60 characters are what search shows). **Description:** keyword in the first two lines, source/licence line, one "Einschlafhilfe" mention. **Tags:** minimal; place keywords in title and description instead **[my judgment]**.
4. **Captions:** skip for sound-only videos; there is no speech to caption.
5. **Chapters:** not needed.

### 3.4 Second and third markets
Only one cross-market number exists in my pull, the country share of searches for "rain sounds for sleeping" [VidIQ]: US 21.5%, Vietnam 18.8%, Indonesia 13.5%, India 3.8%, UK 3.7%, Bangladesh 3.2%, Canada 2.7%, Germany 1.8%, Brazil 1.7%. It is coarse and says nothing about revenue per view (check Studio revenue by country once monetized).

| Order | Market | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Germany, Austria, Switzerland | German titles (File 3 calendar) |
| 2 | English (US, UK, Canada) | **Separate uploads** with English titles, different audio and scene (not a re-title of a German video). The English head term is huge (2.59M/month) but competition is 46.4 and giants own it, so target long-tail English phrases; I have no credits left to pull them |
| 3 | Not established | I cannot validate Japan or Brazil from my data (Brazil is 1.7% of the English term). Check these with VidIQ's Keyword Inspector, set to the country: `som de chuva para dormir`, `sonido de lluvia para dormir`, `雨の音 睡眠`, `bruit de pluie pour dormir`. These translations are mine, not verified in VidIQ |

**Reaching other languages without touching the audio:** in Studio → Content → [video] → Languages, add a translated title and description per language. It is free and changes nothing about the audio. Subtitles add nothing to a sound-only video; auto-dubbing is out of play (§3.3).

## 4. Part 4 — Contrarian and better options

| Idea | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Record your own rain** (window, corrugated/tin roof, heavy downpour) | **Do first** | Original audio answers §0, avoids shared-file Content ID claims, and a tin-roof recording directly matches the 70M-view scene type. Costs only time |
| **Black-screen versions** | **Do** | Only German-specific evidence I found (9.9M views); 0.6 GB file uploads in minutes; saves battery and suits light-sensitive sleepers. Upload as a separate video only with different audio from your main video |
| **Location variants** (Schwarzwald, Berlin) | **Test** | Keyword data not pulled for these; check §3.2 first |
| **Rain + fireplace / distant tram** | **Test** | Evidence from one 48.5M-view fireplace video; only if every layer loops seamlessly (use `acrossfade` per layer, tested mechanism) |
| **Batch length variants** (3 h / 8 h / 10 h) | **Delay** | Free with the pipeline, but they look like minimal variation (§0). Wait until the channel has distinct main videos and check Studio results |
| **Original 3D scene** (Blender) | **Maybe later** | Two large ambience channels use original 3D, but rendering on an i5-4210U is slow and the learning cost is high |
| **German narrated sleep stories** | **Out of scope; note only** | The strongest small-channel breakouts in my German pull, but they are spoken, often AI-voiced and AI-labelled, and the repetition policy applies. A different business than a rain channel |

Other free sources:
- **Freesound** with the licence filter set to **CC0**: no attribution needed. CC-BY needs credit; CC-BY-NC is not allowed for monetized use.
- **YouTube Audio Library** (inside Studio): pre-licensed for YouTube; claim risk is low there. Catalogue is smaller.
- **Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit** for footage; confirm each clip's licence page, keep a spreadsheet of file → URL → licence.
- Keep raw stock clips out of any public repo (File 1, §2).
